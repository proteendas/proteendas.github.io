First time

  1. Install AltServer on a Mac or Windows PC from altstore.io.
  2. Use it to install AltStore on your iPhone, signing in with your own Apple ID.
  3. Open this page on the iPhone and tap Add to AltStore, then install ArthIQ.
  4. First launch: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → trust your Apple ID.

Good to know

  • With a free Apple ID the app must be refreshed every 7 days. AltStore does this automatically when AltServer is on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Up to 3 sideloaded apps at a time per free Apple ID.
  • All trading in ArthIQ is simulated with virtual money. It is an education app, not investment advice.
